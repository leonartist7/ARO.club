## Product and execution decision

Preserve ARO's React/Vite foundations and Tonguee language vertical. Prove one Calgary language Circle from explicit learning goal and contribution through a human-approved opportunity, commitment and recorded outcome. Do not expand into AR, Seasons, wallets, new verticals or a framework rewrite for awards.

This plan implements the founder's September 8 competition directive as a planning/evidence overlay. It does not certify synthetic screens as real functionality or waive I0, Trust, privacy, money or release gates. Runtime packages still trace through AGENTS.md and the existing package specifications. The full P1–P5 loop is not currently a credible date commitment: dates below are targets with explicit go/no-go gates.

## P0 qualification and P1 launch blockers

1. Confirm developer enrollment, account type, production access, signing owner, Mac/iPhone/Android access and first native release history. Founder owns identity, contracts and fees.
2. Choose one primary eligible store based on actual access. Preserve React/Vite; investigate Capacitor as the first packaging candidate. RevenueCat documents a Capacitor SDK, but native compatibility, push, auth links and store-quality acceptance still need a bounded package/spike.
3. New Google personal accounts can require 12 opted-in testers for 14 continuous days before requesting production access. Starting September 8 reaches September 22 before access/review time; starting September 18 reaches October 2. The proposed September 18–20 submission schedule cannot be assumed viable for a new personal account.
4. Close I0 environment matching, Auth/recovery and independent-review evidence. Resolve domain disposition or explicitly approve a controlled alternative support/auth domain. Do not use the unrelated aro.club site as ARO's store support URL.
5. Complete the real core loop, keeping source Trust rules intact. A native wrapper around the current synthetic prototype is not the intended useful product.
6. Establish a real digital paid benefit and RevenueCat purchase/restore/management flow. Keep real-world services and payouts separate. Judge access must work without purchase.
7. Verify production build on devices, store metadata/privacy, account deletion, support and judge access. No device/store check has passed in this audit.
8. Full English Devpost rules fetch timed out repeatedly; indexed rules and organizer submission/category pages were available. Re-open the complete rules before final eligibility assertion or submission; archive the reviewed version and date.
