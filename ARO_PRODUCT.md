# ARO — Product Scope

## V1: validate the opportunity loop through Tonguee

ARO V1 does **not** launch a universal marketplace. It validates the loop in the existing language vertical:

1. A learner expresses a goal or interest.
2. A verified teacher offers an experience.
3. People discover, book, attend, and reflect on it.
4. ARO captures only the outcome evidence needed to improve future matching and trust.

The existing Tonguee experience is therefore the first ARO opportunity type: a language-learning experience led by a verified teacher.

Terminology: an **Opportunity** is the universal arrangement, a **Circle** is the participant cohort/operating group around it, and a Tonguee **Experience** is the language-vertical format. Existing Tonguee UI and tables are not mass-renamed in P0/P1.

## V1 users and jobs

| User | Job ARO helps complete | Existing foundation |
|---|---|---|
| Learner | Find a safe, welcoming way to practice a language in real life | Explore, experiences, booking, Passport |
| Teacher / host | Offer a credible experience and build reputation | Teacher application, verification, dashboard |
| Admin | Keep the marketplace safe and high-quality | Trust Engine, admin panel, audit log |

## Product boundaries for this phase

- Do not introduce public generic intent feeds, bounties, teams, wallet balances, crypto, automated negotiation, or agent-initiated transactions.
- Do not change the verified-teacher publication gate.
- Do not relabel shipped Tonguee UI to ARO until an approved brand and migration package exists.
- New opportunity types require written product, data, trust/safety, and economics specifications.

## Real-world and online hierarchy

ARO’s signature outcome is real-world human activity. Online participation may improve accessibility, preparation, follow-up, or enable a deliberately approved online format; it is not a fallback to an attention feed and must not be counted as physical “real-life hours.” Each outcome metric states whether activity was in-person, live online, or asynchronous.

## Success signals

Prioritize completed, safe experiences; repeat participation; verified host reliability; learner-reported progress; and healthy local density. Treat bookings, attendance, cancellations, support incidents, and repeat behavior as outcome signals—not as permission to create an opaque social score.
