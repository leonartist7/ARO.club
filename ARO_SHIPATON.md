# ARO — Shipathon Scope

## Purpose

This document protects the team from attempting the entire ARO vision during Shipathon. The submission should prove one magical, measurable loop with a coherent mobile-first product.

## Thesis

**Latent demand + human capability → AI-created opportunity → commitment → real-world outcome → verified value.**

## Signature demo

1. A user says what they want.
2. The user shares what they know.
3. ARO finds a legitimate demand signal.
4. ARO says, “People near you want what you know.”
5. ARO creates an understandable opportunity proposal.
6. Participants commit or book under clear terms.
7. The qualified host accepts.
8. A minimum viable ARO Director improves the format.
9. The Circle meets in real life.
10. The host earns through the approved marketplace flow.
11. The outcome is recorded with its evidence source.
12. The permitted graph becomes more useful.

## Must-have submission capabilities

- mobile-first participant and host experience;
- Opportunity Radar;
- explicit Intent and Capability capture;
- privacy-preserving Demand Signals;
- Opportunity proposal and detail;
- Circle cohort/state;
- commitment or booking path with honest terms;
- minimum viable ARO Director guidance;
- existing Trust foundation and verified publishing;
- Proof/outcome capture;
- RevenueCat-powered digital subscription or purchase with verified entitlement state;
- analytics sufficient to support competition and product metrics.

“Must-have” defines the intended submission story, not authorization to implement. Each capability enters through the relevant ARO package and gates in `ARO_BUILD_PLAYBOOK.md`.

## Explicit non-goals

- speculative crypto or native token;
- global marketplace launch;
- pet-care launch;
- licensed-professional marketplace;
- giant social feed or dating;
- autonomous finance or agent-initiated transactions;
- every future ARO opportunity format;
- complex business operating-system integration;
- broad mobile rewrite without the technical gate.

## Competition and product metrics

Track definitions and evidence for users, active hosts, Circles formed/completed, GMV, subscription revenue, verified host earnings, intent → commitment conversion, commitment → attendance conversion, real-life hours generated, and participant → host conversion.

Never inflate metrics with test records, unverified projections, gross payment volume that was refunded, or passive views presented as real-world outcomes.

## Confirmed 2026 competition baseline

RevenueCat’s official July 2, 2026 announcement confirms that an eligible standard submission must be a brand-new iOS or Android app released for the first time to Apple App Store, Google Play, or Samsung Galaxy Store between **August 1 and September 30, 2026**. It must use the RevenueCat SDK for at least one in-app purchase or serve ads through RevenueCat Ads. The post directs entrants to the current DevPost rules for full judging and eligibility details.

Source checked August 11, 2026: `https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/company/announcing-shipaton-2026`.

Before implementation or submission relies on category criteria, team eligibility, ownership, geography, judging, submission evidence, or sponsor awards, verify the current official DevPost rules and record the result in `DECISIONS.md` or a package addendum. The official announcement may be updated, and P0.1 does not freeze every competition rule.

Suggested planning sequence, subject to that verification:

1. P0/P0.1 governance and security preflight.
2. P1–P2 explicit capability/intent foundations.
3. P3 signature opportunity suggestion.
4. P4 commitment and subscription/payment paths.
5. P5 Proof and analytics.
6. Submission polish, rehearsed demo, evidence, and reliability buffer.

P6 adjacent-vertical expansion is not required for the signature demo and should not displace completion quality.

## Mobile strategy

Preserve the existing React/Vite application and responsive investment unless an explicit technical gate demonstrates a rewrite is necessary. Capacitor is a likely packaging direction, not a locked decision. Before choosing it, verify current iOS/Android store policies, RevenueCat requirements, authentication/deep-link behavior, push needs, native plugin support, accessibility, build/release ownership, and competition submission rules.
