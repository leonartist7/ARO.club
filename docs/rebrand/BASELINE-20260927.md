## Asset inventory and art direction

The founder-supplied approved visual reference is preserved at

`5F49515ECBC942C22C1375AA8A8858809D2E8A04177115B58D188E33EE5475E1`).
It is reference evidence, not a production logo master or proof of trademark
rights. The founder supplied it for the approved rebrand; usage beyond this
project has not been independently licensed or verified.

Reusable: `public/fv1/aro-portal-home-*` can support discovery; `aro-shared-stories-table-*` can support connection; `aro-living-miniature-calgary-*` belongs to the labelled spatial preview. Existing Maya portrait is an illustrative persona, never a real host. `public/brand/coco-hero-*` remains vertical-specific. Inspect final crops before reuse.

Planned new assets are screen-tied and conditional on visual need:

| Draft success | Honest editable class draft, no booking implication | 480/960 WebP/AVIF, 1:1 | Materials/outcome; decorative |

Reference palette: orange `#F05A28`, action `#C94320`, yellow `#FFD447`, ivory `#FFF8EE`, charcoal `#252420`, green `#27834A`. Adults have varied recognizably human faces and proportions, clothing consistent across related scenes, believable activity, tactile ceramic/paper/wood/fabric, natural warm lighting and matte surfaces. The yellow portal is an open circular architectural/compositional motif, not a spinner or second logo. Leave UI copy/buttons/prices out of art; no generated person may represent a real host or review. Record prompt, output origin, rights, dimensions, weight, crop and final placement in a versioned manifest. Target first mobile illustration <=250 KB compressed unless a measured exception is documented.
