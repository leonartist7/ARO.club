# Evidence vault

Created 2026-09-08. Keep production claims traceable to dated exports or captures. Do not store secrets, raw customer personal data, judge passwords or payment credentials in Git.

Folders: revenue, downloads, analytics, conversion, retention, RevenueCat, OneSignal, BuildInPublic, community-feedback, design, store, launch, screenshots, demo, experiments.

Use YYYY-MM-DD-description filenames. Each item needs capture time/timezone, environment, app version/build, source/provider, metric definition, cohort/window and exclusions, collector and claim supported. Label test/sandbox data and generated illustrations. A missing artifact means unverified, never zero.

Keep a redacted shareable copy; reference private source exports by controlled location. Obtain permission before publishing identifiable feedback or customer footage. Record numerator and denominator, refunds/currency and immature cohorts. Do not mix marketplace GMV with RevenueCat subscription revenue.
