# Eligibility, release and submission checklist

Research checked September 21, 2026. Unchecked items are not satisfied. This document does not certify entrant eligibility.

## Confirmed official requirements

The deadline is September 30, 2026, 11:45 p.m. PDT (October 1, 08:45 Paris). Formal rules begin July 31; marketing says August 1. A September launch is unaffected. Standard entries need an eligible supported-platform app, its first public eligible-store release in the submission window, US availability, and RevenueCat purchasing or RevenueCat Ads. Prior development is permitted; prior store release is excluded. [Official rules](https://revenuecat-shipaton-2026.devpost.com/rules).

One app can enter compatible categories; only one Influencer category is allowed. No explicit general multiple-prize cap was found; multiple wins are not promised. [Organizer clarification](https://revenuecat-shipaton-2026.devpost.com/forum_topics/44452-is-it-possible-that-i-can-apply-with-one-app-in-mulitple-categories).

Prepare English description, live store URL, RevenueCat project ID, public demo video under two minutes, 1024×1024 icon, at least one 1179×2556 screenshot without a device frame, and premium judge access. TestFlight/testing tracks are not public qualifying releases. Preserve judge access throughout judging; confirm current end date at submission (research record: October 13 noon PDT). [Submission guide](https://www.shipaton.com/blog/how-to-submit-your-app-for-shipaton).
