## Positioning

Turn a language-learning intention into a real Circle.

ARO is being built for adults who want to practise a language with other people and have something to contribute. The intended journey moves from private goals and capabilities to an explainable proposal, human approval, free commitment and an attributable outcome.

ARO explores how language-learning intentions can become small, human-approved Circles. Its current foundations include formation interactions, an application and Trust flow, and an in-review Next.js migration. The latest candidate passes disposable database tests, while complete accessibility and authenticated journey acceptance remain open.

The full P1–P5 loop, native store release and RevenueCat digital Pro integration are still in development. We are separating free real-world participation from optional paid digital tools. Synthetic examples are labelled and do not represent real events or traction.

Use only after P1–P5, native and billing acceptance: ARO helps adults turn a language goal and something they can contribute into a small Circle. People approve the proposal before anyone commits.
