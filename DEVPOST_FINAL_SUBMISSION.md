Name: ARO. Tagline proposal: Turn what you want to learn into something you do together.

## The problem

People have interests they would like to pursue with others, but an interest alone does not provide a host, a group, a place or a workable plan. ARO begins with language practice and the practical step from intention to a shared experience.

## The insight

An opportunity becomes understandable when people can see how their goal, contribution and context fit together. The product should explain that connection and let people approve the next step.
