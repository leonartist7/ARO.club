# ARO — Opportunity Engine

## Purpose

The Opportunity Engine is a future decision-support loop that turns legitimate demand and capability into real-world opportunity. It starts as transparent assistance, not autonomous market control.

## Lifecycle

1. **Signal:** a person explicitly shares an intent or a business/host exposes eligible capacity.
2. **Qualification:** ARO checks consent, category eligibility, trust/safety requirements, and data completeness.
3. **Composition:** ARO may suggest compatible people, time, place, format, capacity, and economics.
4. **Commit:** people and hosts explicitly accept terms; financial commitment follows the dedicated payment spec.
5. **Publish and operate:** only authorized, verified parties publish where a category requires it.
6. **Proof:** attendance, completion, satisfaction, and approved outcome evidence are recorded.
7. **Learn:** aggregate, privacy-preserving evidence may improve future suggestions.

## Guardrails

- Do not present covert inference as fact about intent, identity, safety, or compatibility.
- No automatic outreach, booking, charge, payout, or public disclosure without explicit approval.
- Recommendation quality never overrides Trust policy, RLS, moderation, or human review.
- Show uncertainty honestly; do not promise fill rate, earnings, or personal outcomes.
- High-risk categories need a dedicated policy before automated composition.

When an Opportunity becomes operational, its participating group is the Circle. In Tonguee, the published vertical format remains an Experience. Qualification follows `ARO_TRUST_SAFETY.md`; economics follows `ARO_MONEY.md`.
